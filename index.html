The Choco Hub Philosophy

From Bean to Bar, Crafted With Passion

Founded in the verdant foothills of the Western Ghats, Choco Hub began as a humble kitchen experiment by pastry chefs devoted to highlighting Indian single-origin cacao. Today, we work directly with sustainable farmers to ferment, roast, and stone-grind beans into world-class chocolates without preservatives or artificial emulsifiers.
